## What he is looking for

Work where the hard part is the system rather than the ticket. He is most interested in teams
building AI-native products, developer tools, or platforms where correctness and cost both matter,
and where an engineer is expected to own a problem end to end rather than implement someone else's
answer to it. He has spent most of his time working without a designer or a senior reviewer, so he
is comfortable with ambiguity and would rather join a team where that is normal than one where it
is a warning sign.

## Education

He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Data Science from the University of

## Visa sponsorship

He is on an H-1B that is transferable to a new employer: they file a transfer petition directly.
That is not a fresh cap-subject petition, and it does not wait on the annual H-1B lottery, so no
new visa sponsorship is required. His work authorisation does not hold up a start date the way
starting sponsorship from scratch would.

## Location and remote

He is based in San Francisco. He is open to fully remote roles, but prefers work that is fully
onsite or hybrid.

## Availability

He is available to start immediately: no notice period, no other personal constraint on his end
that would push out a start date. The one variable is timing on the H-1B transfer petition a new
employer would need to file, and the actual start date follows however long that takes. That
timeline is set by the petition, not by him.
